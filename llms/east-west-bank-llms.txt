# East West Bank

> East West Bank is the California state-chartered commercial banking subsidiary of East West Bancorp, Inc. (NASDAQ: EWBC), headquartered in Pasadena, California, with roughly $79.7 billion in total assets and more than 125 locations across the United States and Greater China. A Member FDIC institution and one of the few U.S. banks holding a full banking license in China, it specializes in cross-border U.S.-China commercial banking, treasury, and Global Transaction Services (GTS). Its first-party developer program, "Bridge Open Banking," exposes commercial banking APIs to manage sub-accounts, retrieve balances, transfer funds, and obtain commercial account information. Access is gated behind sign-in and sales onboarding, so no OpenAPI/Swagger is publicly downloadable.

## APIs

- [East West Bank Bridge Open Banking API](https://apiportal.eastwestbank.com/how-it-works): First-party commercial banking API program for GTS clients — manage sub-accounts, retrieve balances, transfer funds, and obtain commercial account information. OAuth 2.0 authorization; sandbox with live test accounts. Specs gated behind sign-in.

## Auth

- OAuth 2.0. Create an application to obtain a client id and secret; base-64 encode them and exchange for a short-lived access token presented on API calls. HTTP 401 on mismatched credentials or an expired/invalidated token.

## Sandbox

- Sandbox with provider-supplied live test accounts. Open and manage test accounts, obtain account/transaction information, transfer funds, and retrieve statements. In-browser "try it" testing on the portal (no client certificate required for portal access).
